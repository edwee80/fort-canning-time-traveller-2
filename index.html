FIELD NOTES & VISITOR ESSENTIALS

Before you set off

⌖

Explore responsibly

Follow existing park paths, respect heritage structures, and do not enter restricted areas. Check park opening information before visiting and stay on public paths. Photography is optional for the historical chapter quizzes.

◷

Best time & pacing

Visit between 7:30 am – 10:30 am or 4:30 pm – 6:30 pm for cooler weather, golden photography light, and shorter queues at the Tree Tunnel. Allow 60–75 minutes for the full 7-stop trail.

◈

Transit & access points

Start from Dhoby Ghaut MRT (Exit B) for the Tree Tunnel on the northern slopes (mostly downhill walk after Stop 2), or Fort Canning MRT (Exit B) for Jubilee Park on the western foothills (outdoor escalators carry you uphill).

🎟

Admission & Battlebox tickets

Fort Canning Park is 100% free and open 24 hours daily. The exterior of the Battlebox bunker is free to visit and stamp; entering the underground rooms requires a timed-entry ticket.

💧

Restrooms, water & shelter

Public restrooms, water refill stations, and rain shelters are located at Fort Canning Centre (hilltop plateau), Jubilee Park, and near the Battlebox Visitor Centre.

📸

Camera & offline guide

All photo checks happen privately on your device—no images are uploaded. Download your route's 8-page PDF Field Guide before setting off so you have the full map offline.
